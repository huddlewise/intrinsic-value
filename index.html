A few notes on what this tool does—and what it doesn’t.

It’s an estimate of the value of a business based on the cash it may generate for owners over time. Because future cash flows are uncertain, this tool shows a range rather than claiming there is one exact answer.

You provide every input. The calculator does not connect to market feeds, fetch company filings, or transmit your entries. Verify figures against current primary sources before using them.
